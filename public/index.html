    Combat

    • Click fast. Classic combat: no attack cooldown. After a hit, a target resists damage for half a second.
    • Sprint-hit & W-tap. Sprint hits knock enemies much further. Double-tap sprinters must let go of W and tap again.
    • Rod. The fishing rod deals no damage but knocks back — use it to start or reset combos.
    • Crits. Hit while falling for 1.5× damage.
    • Block. Hold right-click with the sword to take half damage.
    • Bow. Hold to draw; a full draw hits hardest.
    • Golden flask. Fast regeneration plus two bonus hearts.
    • Falls hurt (except the drop from spawn). Kills restock arrows, blocks and flasks.
    • Kills earn XP and coins. Level up, then spend coins on skins, capes and ranks in the Wardrobe.

    Modes

    • Arena FFA. The full kit: sword, rod, bow, flasks and blocks.
    • Pot PvP. No natural regen. Look down and throw Healing Splash potions at your feet, drink Swiftness, and throw Void Pearls to teleport.
    • Sumo FFA. Fists only and no damage. Knock players off the ring.
    • Combo FFA. Tiny hit delay and heavy armor. Chain hits together.
